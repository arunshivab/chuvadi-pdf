feat(rendering): Type 3 font rendering on raster and SVG sinks

Render Type 3 font glyphs (CharProc content streams mapped to text space
by the font matrix) on both the raster and SVG display-list pipelines.

- Add d0/d1 to IContentOperatorSink and dispatch them in the content stream
  walker; Type3Font parses FontMatrix, Encoding/Differences to CharProcs,
  Resources, and per-glyph widths.
- Raster: each cached glyph is placed via NestedDisplayListOp with the
  composition FontMatrix * textScale * Tm * CTM.
- SVG: a new Type3UseOp renders as a transform group wrapping the cached
  glyph sub-display-list.
- d1 uncoloured glyphs paint with the text fill colour (colour operators
  suppressed); d0 coloured glyphs keep their own colour. Glyphs are cached
  per font, code, and fill colour.
- Tests: RasterType3Tests (pixel-verified) and SvgType3Tests (structural).

Roadmap item 26.
