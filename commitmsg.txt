Wire /JBIG2Globals through the decode pipeline

Adds FilterParameters.Jbig2Globals and makes ContentStreamLoader resolve a
filter's /JBIG2Globals shared-segment stream (dereferenced and decoded) when a
resolver is available, so JBIG2 images that reference a globals symbol
dictionary decode from a real PDF. Both display-list builders pass the resolver
at image and soft-mask decode sites; Jbig2Filter forwards the globals to the
decoder.

Verified end-to-end: the public Norconex sample PDF decodes to a byte-exact
352x91 image through the full pipeline, covered by an end-to-end regression
test loading the sample PDF. Marks roadmap #22 (JBIG2 decode) done.
